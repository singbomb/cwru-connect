# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User selected a proposed Next.js + TypeScript + Supabase implementation plan on October 27, 2026. Provider contracts, production schema, package versions, email delivery, and deployment remain technical decisions. The local demonstration uses dependency-free JavaScript and synthetic JSON; it is separate from the future application.

## Users

CWRU undergraduates, including beginners without resumes, discover research and track outreach. Verified CWRU lab owners and explicitly delegated recruiters manage openings, review applications, and respond. Scoped operators curate and verify records.

## Product Purpose

Help students identify plausible research opportunities and obtain visible, substantive follow-through. Reduce repeated application work and recruiting administration. Actual starts and human responses are separate from clicks, automated receipts, offers, and accepted offers.

## Positioning

The PRD proposes current local recruiting information, practical-fit discovery, delegated recruiting, and response visibility as hypotheses to validate. It does not establish category novelty, improved placements, or institutional sponsorship.

## Operating Context

Responsive student phone and recruiter desktop journeys. Labs belong to CWRU schools, including the School of Medicine; standalone hospitals and other universities are outside R1. Affiliation, undergraduate status, and lab authority require distinct checks. Source curation must minimize PI effort and requires permission where applicable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

R1 includes the lab/publication catalog, current openings, optional resume-assisted private profiles, search, saves/comparison, applications, recruiter messaging, and distinct lab/opening and verified PI/recruiter recommendation feeds. Catalog presence is not an opening. Recruiting labels distinguish confirmed opening, accepting inquiries, not recruiting, and unknown. Student peers and mentorship reviews remain R2 with separate gates.

Confirmed profile facts and explicit preferences drive recommendations; unconfirmed resume extraction does not. Hard constraints precede ranking. Names, photos, contact details, sensitive traits, GPA, private messages, prestige, and popularity do not score. Explanations expose evidence and unknowns without promising selection or showing a match probability.

No automated applications or candidate rejections. Resume deletion revokes file access and future matching use; submitted factual snapshots have a distinct lifecycle. Production retention periods, operational thresholds, and calibrated ranking weights remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The source PRD uses the working name CWRU Research Connect. The local project folder is Case Connect. Final naming, institutional branding permission, logo, typography, and production visual identity are undecided. For the local demonstration, the user selected the university catalog concept and requested a sharper LinkedIn/Indeed style. All authored content is English.

## Product Principles

- Show recruiting uncertainty and source freshness where decisions happen.
- Give no-resume beginners a useful path.
- Make suggestions understandable and correctable.
- Preserve private information and scoped authority.
- Measure substantive response and actual starts separately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The PRD targets WCAG 2.2 AA for implementation. Keyboard and touch operation, visible focus, labeled controls, recoverable validation, and responsive layouts are required. A local visual inspection cannot establish full conformance.
