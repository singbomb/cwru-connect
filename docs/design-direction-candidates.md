# Demo direction candidates

Planning record for the Impeccable direction workshop. No product identity is final until selected. Mode: Operate. Mechanism: explicit profile evidence plus practical constraints leads to relevant, actionable research discovery. Scene: a student comparing research options between classes. The standard recruiting dashboard and its decorative science-fiction opposite are the category rut.

1. **Course catalog** — University catalog pages; navy, parchment, open sans and serif course titles; precise field labels.

2. **Research article annotations** — Article margins; black, white, oxblood, serif hierarchy; evidence anchors.

3. **Campus wayfinding** — Building signs; evergreen, cream, humanist sans; accessible status labels.

4. **Annotated reading list** — Library reading lists; plum, pale lavender, warm paper, literary serif; evidence attached to each result.

5. **Archive accession guide** — Archive labels; umber and cream, serif titles and compact metadata; canonical identifiers.

6. **Exhibition wall text** — Museum labels; ultramarine and white, clear grotesk; deliberate comparative hierarchy.

7. **Alumni bulletin** — Campus bulletin; brick and warm white, editorial serif; short useful summaries.

Assigned candidate: 4, annotated reading list. Seed: seed:24bcc867a9755486. All catalog challengers were considered against audience identification and product clarity and declined; their discipline raises are recorded in the decision payload. The app retains standard web controls, navigation, and result structure in every option. A selected style applies to the local demo only; the production identity remains open.
